What we do

From natural fibre to finished piece.

GS Artisan manufactures a wide range of handicraft products using 100% natural and sustainable materials, including seagrass, water hyacinth, jute, date leaf, kans grass, palm fiber, and corn husk or corn leaf.

Our products combine traditional craftsmanship with practical, contemporary designs, bringing the natural beauty and unique textures of these materials into homes, interiors, retail collections and lifestyle spaces.
